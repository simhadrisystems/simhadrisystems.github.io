eSudoku

Simple. Classic. Professional.

A clean, distraction-free Sudoku puzzle app for Android, built for players who want a genuine logic challenge — not a lucky guess. Every puzzle is generated using real human solving techniques and verified to have exactly one solution reachable through pure deduction, so what you're solving is always fair.

See what's inside

Why eSudoku is different

Most Sudoku apps just remove random numbers from a solved grid. eSudoku doesn't.

No guessing, ever

Every board is checked against real solving techniques — from naked and hidden singles, through pairs, triples, and box-line reduction, up to advanced X-Wing, swordfish, and chain techniques — before it's allowed to reach your screen. Whatever difficulty you pick, the puzzle is guaranteed solvable step by step, using logic alone.
