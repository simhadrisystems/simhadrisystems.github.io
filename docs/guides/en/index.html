2. Installing the App

  1. Open Google Play Store on your Android phone
  2. Search for WinEntry or WinEntry Simple Inventory
  3. Tap the result published by Simhadri Systems → tap Install
  4. Once installed, tap Open

Requirements: Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later · A Google account for sign-in

Updates: The Play Store notifies you automatically when a new version is released. You can also check from inside the app: Settings → Check for Update.
↑ Back to top

3. Signing In

WinEntry uses your Google account to sign in — the same account you use for Gmail or YouTube.

  1. Open WinEntry — the Sign In screen appears
  2. Tap Sign in with Google
  3. Select your Google account from the list
  4. Grant the permissions requested
  5. You will be taken to the Home screen
Always use the same Google account. Signing in with a different account will not show your previous data.

4. Getting Started — First-Time Setup

When you open the app after signing in for the first time, an amber Getting Started card appears at the top of the Home screen. Tap this card to open the setup checklist.

Language toggle: The English / తెలుగbuttons at the top of this dialog switch the app language immediately. You can change it again at any time from Settings.

Step 1 — Business Info

  1. Tap Set Up next to "Set Up Business Information"
  2. Fill in: Owner Name, Business Name (required), Licence / Reg. No, Address, Phone
  3. Tap Save & Register → confirm in the dialog
The "Account:" line at the top shows your Google sign-in email — it is read-only.

Step 2 — Import Product List

Downloads the admin's standard product master list to your phone.

  1. Tap Download next to "Import Product List"
  2. Wait 5–30 seconds — a green tick appears when complete
If "Request Access" appears instead of "Download": Your account has not been activated by the admin yet. Tap "Request Access" to notify the admin. Once approved, come back and tap Download.

Step 3 — Load Sample Data (Optional)

Loads dummy data so you can practise before entering real figures. Skip this step if you have real opening stock to enter — go directly to Step 4.

  1. Tap Load next to "Import Sample / Test Data"
  2. Select the opening balance date → tap OK

Step 4 — Setup Opening Stock

Enter the actual unit count per product and size as your starting inventory. Two ways to do this:

Option A — Manual entry

  1. Tap Enter next to "Setup Opening Stock (manual)"
  2. For each product, enter units per size column
  3. Tap Save

Option B — Import from Excel

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu → Download Template — an Excel file saves to your phone
  2. Fill in the template on your computer
  3. Transfer back to your phone → ⋮ menu → Import from Excel
