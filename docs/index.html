Our Products

Three apps, two very different jobs

Two keep a shop's stock and sales honest. The third keeps your mind sharp. All three are built the same way — offline-first, no clutter, no nonsense.

Android · Retail Software

eCalc

A fast, no-fuss calculator built for the shop sale counter, not general arithmetic. Pack-size keys turn a unit count into a case total in one tap, a running total and independent memory stay visible at all times, and a full transaction tape logs every calculation of the session as a real audit trail — with true left-to-right math, no textbook order of operations.

Android · Puzzle

eSudoku

A free, offline Sudoku puzzle app with six genuine difficulty levels — Easy through Grandmaster — plus a 500-level Journey mode, Daily Challenges, and achievements. Every puzzle is generated using real human solving techniques and verified solvable by logic alone, with no guessing required.

Android · Retail Software

WinEntry

A specialized retail inventory and accountability tool, originally engineered for licensed wine and liquor retailers in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. WinEntry automates daily opening-vs-closing stock reconciliation with unit-level tracking, a bilingual English/Telugu interface, and a privacy-first, offline-by-default design — adaptable to any business that manages stock by count and size.

Our Story

Two sides of Simhadri Systems

We started by solving a real, unglamorous problem for real businesses. We're now applying the same care to something people do for fun.

Where we started

Business tools, built for the shop floor

Simhadri Systems began with WinEntry — practical, offline-first retail software solving a genuine daily problem for licensed wine and liquor retailers: honest, accurate stock reconciliation, in a bilingual interface built for the people who actually use it. eCalc extends that same shop-floor focus to the sale counter itself — a calculator built around pack sizes and running totals instead of general arithmetic.

eSudoku is our first step into brain-training entertainment — a Sudoku app built with the same standards as our business tools: no shortcuts, no filler, puzzles that are genuinely fair because they're solvable by logic alone.

About

Who's behind Simhadri Systems

Simhadri Systems builds software with two different audiences in mind: business owners who need tools that just work, and puzzle players who want a genuine challenge. WinEntry came first, built to solve a real day-to-day problem for retail stock management, and eCalc followed to solve the counter-side half of that same problem. eSudoku is the newer side of that same philosophy, applied to something people do for fun rather than for work.
