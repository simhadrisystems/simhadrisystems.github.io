WinEntry

Specialized Retail Inventory & Accountability Tool

Originally engineered by Simhadri Systems to meet the rigorous daily stock requirements of licensed retail wine and liquor shops in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, WinEntry is a robust, versatile tool designed for any business that relies on precise, unit-based inventory tracking.

What the App Does

WinEntry automates the essential daily reconciliation process — the "opening vs. closing" balance — that is critical for high-volume retail environments.

  • Unit-Level Tracking Effortlessly manage inventory across multiple brands, categories and varied sizes (e.g., bottles, cases or packets).
  • Daily Reconciliation Log daily purchases, sales and expenses to generate instant, accurate daily stock sheets.
  • Bilingual & Adaptive A flexible interface (currently supporting English and Telugu) that can be localized to any language with minimal technical changes.
  • Privacy-First Design Operates fully offline by default to keep your business data local, with an optional, secure cloud backup for users who require multi-device syncing.

Why It's Valuable

  • Built by the Industry, for the Industry Born from the unique needs of retail licensees, WinEntry is offered as a collaborative tool. Since licensees operate in exclusive geographic zones, the app serves as a professional-grade solution shared among peers, fostering a community of feedback and continuous improvement.
  • Eliminates Human Error By replacing tedious manual ledger entries with automated digital logging, the app significantly reduces the risk of mathematical errors and financial discrepancies.
  • Highly Customizable While it excels in the liquor industry, the underlying logic is code-neutral: it can be seamlessly adapted to any retail sector — such as dairy, hardware or cosmetics — without requiring code changes.

Generalized Business Use Cases

The app's framework is suited to any business where stock is managed by count and size, including:

  • Dairy & Beverage Tracking crates and individual bottles with high daily turnover.
  • Construction & Hardware Managing bags of cement, boxes of tiles or plumbing fixtures.
  • Personal Care & Cosmetics Organizing multiple brands across various SKU sizes (ml/grams).
  • Pet Supply Stores Tracking packaged food bags and accessories by unit.
